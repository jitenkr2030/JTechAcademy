About JTech Professional Learning Centre

JTech LMS is an online + mobile learning platform built to deliver high-quality technical education across India — from tier-1 cities with great bandwidth, to tier-2 and tier-3 towns where reliable internet is still a challenge.

Our Mission

Make industry-ready skills accessible to every learner in India at affordable monthly fees — without compromising on quality, depth, or career outcomes.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted learning platform for technical and professional skills, serving 10,000+ students with verified certificates and direct industry pathways.

Key Features

  • Dual-bandwidth video — HD streaming for tier-1, low-bandwidth + downloadable videos for tier-2/3
  • Installable mobile app — works on Android & iOS via PWA, study anywhere
  • Strictly monthly billing — no big upfront payment, auto-recurring subscriptions
  • Verified certificates — auto-issued on completion with QR-verifiable links
  • 6 structured pathways — combine courses into complete career tracks
  • Live + self-paced — mix of recorded video lessons and scheduled live classes
  • Hackathon prep tracks — specialised preparation for NASA Space Apps & Smart India Hackathon 2027
